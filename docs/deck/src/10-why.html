Battery on a laptop that is the only interface

Cost to a student
Per-minute billing for speech, plus vision and language calls.Whisper API at $0.006/min: three hours a day is about ₹2,700 a month, speech alone (₹84/$)
Zero after the laptop.no subscription, no API keys
Trade-offs we accept

4-billion-parameter models reason less well than frontier cloud models, so common commands bypass them through a fixed grammar and every model action is validated. Each generative model takes 10 s to load and 5 to 6 GB of RAM. Whisper base mishears in noisy rooms; fragments are dropped rather than acted on.
