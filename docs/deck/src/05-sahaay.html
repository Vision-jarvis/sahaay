04 · What Sahaay does

Two modes, one on-device brain: hands-free control and a screen narrator.

Hands-free mode

Head moves the cursor. Voice types and commands.

For motor impairment, injury, RSI
  • Head cursor: nudge past a dead zone and the cursor moves; hold still and it stays. Calibrates itself in one second.
  • Clicks without hands: short blink = left click, long blink = right click, dwell 0.9 s = click, mouth open = drag.
  • Dictation into any application, English and Hindi.
  • 25 instant commands (click, scroll, press ctrl+s, next window, open Chrome) in under a millisecond.
  • Free-form commands through the language model: "open my downloads and sort by date"
  • Physical switches: any Arduino becomes a click and push-to-talk interface.
Narrator mode

The screen and the camera, described in speech.

For low vision and blindness
  • "what's on my screen" the UI Automation tree summarised in two sentences: which app, what content, what matters.
  • "read this" the focused paragraph or field, read aloud.
  • "describe the picture" a vision-language model explains photos, charts, canvases, scanned forms.
  • "what do you see" the webcam view: people, objects, labels, signs.
  • Every action is confirmed by voice, so the user always knows what just happened.
  • English and Hindi voices, synthesised on the laptop.
