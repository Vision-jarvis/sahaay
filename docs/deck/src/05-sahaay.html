Meet Sahaay

Two modes, one on-device brain: hands-free control and a screen narrator.

Hands-free

Head moves the cursor. Voice types and commands.

For Arjun: motor impairment, injury, RSI
  • Nudge your head, the cursor moves. Hold still, it stays. Calibrates itself.
  • Blink to click, hold still to click, open your mouth to drag.
  • Dictate into any app, in English or Hindi.
  • Say it: "open my downloads", "write a thank-you note to my teacher and save it."
  • Or press a switch. Any Arduino becomes a button interface.
Narrator

The screen and the camera, described in speech.

For Meena: low vision, blindness
  • "What's on my screen?" Two spoken sentences: which app, what content, what matters.
  • "Read this." The paragraph under focus, in her language.
  • "Describe the picture." A vision model explains photos, charts, canvases.
  • "What do you see?" The webcam view, labels and signs, read aloud.
  • Every action is confirmed by voice, so she always knows what just happened.
