Deployment and accessibility

One command installs it on any Snapdragon X or X2 PC. After that it never needs the internet.

# any Snapdragon X / X2 PC · HP OmniBook, EliteBook · no admin rights
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File install.ps1
# Python if missing · venv · models for your chipset, once · espeak-ng · Start Menu shortcut

Private by construction

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Camera frames, audio and screenshots stay in RAM and are discarded. A local log file for support, nothing else written.

Fits the OmniBook a judge owns

Hands-free mode within 16 GB. Both generative models resident on 32 GB (5.4 + 6.1 GB measured), or the 2B vision model on 16 GB. 45 TOPS is enough; 80 TOPS has headroom.

Usable without sight or hands

Global hotkeys, a tray icon, a heads-up panel that shows what was heard and done, spoken confirmation of every action, English and Hindi voices, and switch access through any Arduino, including the UNO Q from the AI Lab kit.
