01 · Who this is for

Two groups of people who cannot use a laptop the way it ships.

Motor impairment

Cannot use a mouse or a keyboard

Cerebral palsy, spinal injury, ALS, severe RSI, a broken arm. The screen works; the hands do not.

Today: every assignment, every portal, every chat needs someone else's hands, or a ₹1.5 lakh eye-gaze system.
Low vision and blindness

Cannot see the screen

Diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, congenital blindness. A screen reader lists forty buttons; it cannot describe a photo, a chart, a scanned form, or the room.

Today: anything that is an image, a canvas or the physical world needs someone else's eyes.
